## My Favorite Series: *The 100*
 
**The 100** is a sci-fi drama series that aired on The CW from 2014 to 2020. It follows a group of young people sent from a space station back to a post-apocalyptic Earth, where they must fight to survive.
 
### Why I Like It
 
- Exciting survival storylines
- Strong, memorable characters
- Hard moral choices in every season
